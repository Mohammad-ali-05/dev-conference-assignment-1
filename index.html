Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before attending DevConf 2026.

What is included with my conference ticket?

Every ticket includes full access to all conference days, keynote presentations, technical sessions, networking opportunities, and coffee breaks. Depending on your ticket tier, you'll also receive access to hands-on workshops, session recordings, exclusive networking events, and additional premium benefits.

Will the conference sessions be recorded?

Yes. All registered attendees will receive access to recorded keynote presentations and technical sessions after the event. Recordings will be available for 30 days, allowing you to revisit your favorite talks or catch sessions you couldn't attend live.

Can I upgrade my ticket after purchasing?

Absolutely. You can upgrade your ticket to a higher tier at any time before the conference, subject to availability. You'll only need to pay the price difference between your current ticket and the new ticket.

What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Tickets are fully refundable up to 30 days before the conference. After that period, tickets are non-refundable, but you may transfer your registration to another attendee by contacting our support team before the event begins.

Who should attend DevConf 2026?

DevConf 2026 is designed for software engineers, web and mobile developers, DevOps professionals, cloud engineers, AI and machine learning practitioners, engineering managers, and computer science students. Whether you're just starting your career or have years of industry experience, you'll find valuable sessions, hands-on workshops, and networking opportunities tailored to every skill level.
